## 2026-09-24 — synthEmitter needs the mains frequency
- **WP:** WP13
- **Change:** `export function synthEmitter(kind: EmitterKindId, variant: number, sampleRate: number, seconds: number, mainsHz: 50 | 60 = 60): Float32Array[];` (additive trailing optional parameter; §5 WP13 "Exported API")
- **Rationale:** the §5 WP13 recipes make PHONE (UK/US cadence "by mains setting"), BUZZ (2×mains harmonics) and MACHINE (50/60 Hz harmonics) depend on `Settings.mainsHz`, but the frozen signature has no mains input (synthHum has one). Workaround until merged: encode mains in `variant` inside WP13.
- **Consumers affected:** WP13 only (src/audio/dsp/emitters.ts, tests/audio/*)

## 2026-09-24 — AudioSystem has no quality hook
- **WP:** WP13
- **Change:** add `setQuality(q: QualityConfig): void;` to `AudioSystem` in src/core/runtime.ts (additive)
- **Rationale:** `q.humVoices` and `q.hrtf` are read only in `createAudioSystem(bus, settings, q)`; every other runtime system (streamer, lighting, post, materials) has `setQuality`, so a runtime quality change (settings panel, `qualityAuto`) cannot reach audio. Workaround until merged: keep the creation-time values.
- **Consumers affected:** WP13 (AudioEngine.ts), WP14 (App quality change wiring)

## 2026-09-25 — Spec clarifications (no signature change; for orchestrator review)
- **WP:** WP13
- **Change 1 (flicker alignment):** flicker transients start at `ctxTime + 0.03 + (t_event − now) − ctx.outputLatency`, and the per-frame hum/buzz flicker automation samples `flicker()` at the frame's sim time and is applied at `ctxTime + 0.03 − outputLatency`. This differs from the §5 WP13 formula only by the `− outputLatency` term (clamped to 0–200 ms; the start time is never in the past). Audio scheduled at ctx time T is heard at T + outputLatency, so subtracting it makes the sound arrive when the frame showing the flicker is on screen, which is the "sample-aligned with the visuals" requirement. When outputLatency is 0 the result is the spec formula exactly. Implemented in `src/audio/env.ts` (`alignedTime`).
- **Change 2 (door rattle):** every `interact` on a DOOR_LEAF plays `doorRattle`. It is louder (0.5) inside, or within 1 cell of, a LOCKED_EXIT landmark and softer (0.32) elsewhere. The only other DOOR_LEAF props are the ENDLESS_HALL fake doors, which do not open either, so without a sound the interact would give no feedback at all. The spec names only the LOCKED_EXIT case. Reject this and WP13 will play nothing outside LOCKED_EXIT.
- **Change 3 (additive stats):** `audio.stats()` returns `AudioStats` plus `outRmsDb` and `outPeakDb`, the post-limiter output level over the last 10 s in dBFS, with −120 meaning silence or not yet measured. It is measured with an AnalyserNode sampled at 4 Hz. Every 10 s `recentEvents()` logs `output rms X dBFS, peak Y dBFS`, and it logs `output WARNING: …` on non-finite samples or a peak above 0 dBFS. The object is still assignable to `AudioStats`. WP14 QA may assert on these fields: for example, output peak ≤ −1 dBFS and RMS above −60 dBFS at the L0 spawn.
- **Consumers affected:** none required (optional for WP14 QA).
